# Develoop Brand Guidelines

## Logo Philosophy

The Develoop logo embodies the concept of the **"Development Loop"**: a continuous cycle the next site can reuse.

### The Loop Concept

The central mark is an abstract infinity-style loop that represents:
- **Continuous improvement**: the same standard, refined as each site is delivered
- **A reusable system**: briefs, protocols and templates a team can start from
- **An adaptive process**: learning at each gate, then writing it back into the library
- **A closed loop**: the work returns to the standard, rather than starting from a blank page

### Design Elements

- **The Loop**: The flowing, interconnected form suggests movement, progress, and the cyclical nature of development
- **Architectural Corners**: The four corner brackets ground the organic loop in structure and precision
- **Flow Arrows**: Subtle directional cues emphasizing the continuous, forward-moving nature of the process
- **Letterspacing**: Premium 0.08em tracking conveys sophistication and intentionality

## Clear Space

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the loop mark on all sides.

```
        [clear space]
             ↓
    ←  [LOOP]  →
       [height]
             ↑
        [clear space]
```

---

## Logo Don'ts

❌ **Don't** stretch or distort the logo  
❌ **Don't** change the color of the wordmark elements  
❌ **Don't** rotate the logo  
❌ **Don't** place on busy or low-contrast backgrounds  
❌ **Don't** add effects (shadows, bevels, gradients beyond brand)  
❌ **Don't** change the letterspacing  
❌ **Don't** use the icon without the wordmark unless space constrained  
Do not break the loop flow. The continuity is essential to the concept.

## Typography

### Primary Typeface
**Canela** (or fallback: Playfair Display, Georgia)
- Used for: Logo wordmark, headlines, display text
- Weights: 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium)

### Secondary Typeface
**Inter**
- Used for: Body text, UI elements, captions
- Weights: 300 (Light), 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (SemiBold)

## Brand Voice

- **Sophisticated** but approachable
- **Expert** without being arrogant
- **Precise** yet human
- **Premium** but not pretentious

### Core Message
> Development is not a straight line. It is a continuous loop of briefing, documentation, learning and refinement.
